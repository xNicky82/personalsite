How every number on this page is built

The pipeline

On load, HubSpot returns the live membership and stage of the Oura Ring Promo — Demos/Deals list. Those deal IDs go straight into three Snowflake queries that build the table, the daily cohort series and the SAL roster, and every ratio and dollar figure is worked out in the page from those results plus the ring price above.

Segment

The deal owner's HubSpot team, strictly: Commercial In-House 1/2, Enterprise 1/2/3/EMEA, SMB Law. Anything else is Other/Unassigned, which has no benchmark because the benchmark is built per team.

Booked · Outstanding · Dropped · Held

Booked is a deal with at least one meeting dated after 27 Aug, any outcome. Held has a meeting marked completed. Outstanding has nothing held, a future meeting still on the books and no closed-lost. Dropped has nothing held and nothing pending, or the deal is closed lost. The three add back to Booked exactly.

SALs, Trials, Trials Secured

SALs are deals with a SAL date, and the number is clickable to see who they are. Trials are deals that entered Trialing in the warehouse or sit in Trialing in live HubSpot, which bridges the sync lag and only ever ratchets up. Trials Secured are in Trial Secured but not yet Trialing, and feed no ratio.

Cost

Ad spend is Facebook spend since 28 Aug on Oura creatives only. Each segment carries its share of booked meetings' worth of that spend, plus the ring price for every meeting held. $/SAL and $/Trial divide that by SALs or Trials.

Picking a day

A day filters the table to the demos booked on that day, and narrows ad spend to that day's Facebook spend, so the cost columns stay honest. The benchmark columns never change, because they are mature cohorts rather than anything happening this week.

Team benchmarks

Mature cohorts only, Jan 2025 to May 2026. Conversion of booked is SALs over leads; conversion of held is measured among leads that actually held a meeting. Paid $/SAL and $/Trial use attributed paid-media cost shares over paid-attributed outcomes, so it is paid media, not blended CAC.

The Organizations tab

Ranked on the signals HubSpot already carries: ICP tier 1 or 2 first, then target accounts, then existing customers by ARR, then headcount. Its cohort is everyone who filled the promo form, so an account appears at Contact before it ever books. Every applicant is named and linked into HubSpot: the link opens their deal when there is one, and their contact record when the account has not booked a demo yet. Every column sorts, and each of the five dates in the track sorts on its own, so you can order by when an account arrived, when it booked, when it held or when it started a trial. Blanks always sink to the bottom. The day chips on the funnel table also narrow it, on the day the account came in.

Freshness

HubSpot and Snowflake are both re-queried every 60 seconds. The warehouse itself pulls from HubSpot and the ad platforms about hourly, so the live pulse panel leads and the table settles behind it. Deals flagged spam or closed lost for Spam/Litigator are dropped throughout, which matters on a giveaway.
